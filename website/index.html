Trybes

Everyone's kilometres, one bar.

A Trybe is a group with a goal. Public ones anyone can join, private ones need an invite, and what happens inside stays with the members.

  • Creators and captains. Captains edit the Trybe, schedule events and remove members. Only the creator can delete it.
  • Its own feed and chat. A post shared into a Trybe appears for members only, on the Trybe and in their feed.
  • Hidden workouts stay hidden. Someone who hides their training simply is not counted on the board.

Cliques

Progress

Proof that you kept going.

Two years of history, drawn two ways, with the goals you set rather than ones picked for you.

  • Calendar by count. One session is a light tint, two to four darker, five or more solid.
  • Seven badge categories, from distance and consistency to exploration and performance.
  • Kilometres or miles, one switch on your account that follows you to a new phone.
Apps and devices

Your watch is already compatible.

Connect Apple Health or Health Connect once and the workouts your watch records arrive on their own. Every brand writes into one of those two stores through its own app, so there is no list to pick from.

Apple Watch Garmin Fitbit Whoop Polar Samsung Oura Wear OS

Workouts, not step counts

Each session arrives with its activity, time, distance and calories. Everyday step totals stay out of your history.

Never counted twice

A run you recorded in Fitrybe is not added again when your watch writes the same session to the health store.

Filed on the right day

A Saturday session synced on Monday still counts for Saturday in your calendar, streak and Trybe goal.

Privacy and safety

You decide who sees you move.

Visibility is checked every time somebody tries to read something, not stamped on when you post. Hide your workouts and your whole history goes with them; switch it back and everything returns exactly as it was.

Workout visibility

One switch hides every workout you have logged, older ones included, from profiles and leaderboards.

Audience per post

Everyone, or your Trybes only. A post shared into a Trybe never leaves it.

Blocking both ways

Posts, comments, profiles, search, follows, messages and notifications all stop at once. They are not told.

Reports that reach a person

Report a post in two taps. It is hidden from you immediately and sent for review.

Being found

Turn off discovery and you stay out of search and suggestions, without hiding from people you know.

Leaving properly

Delete your account in the app. Posts, workouts, messages and memberships go with it.

FAQ

Questions worth answering

Can I get it yet?
Not quite. Fitrybe is in development and going through App Store and Play Store review before general release. Nothing here is a store listing.
What does it cost?
Recording, Trybes, Cliques, the feed and your whole history are the app. A Pro plan is planned as a monthly or annual subscription with a 14-day trial, and pricing is not settled. There are no ads.
What is the difference between a Trybe and a Clique?
A Trybe is a club that lasts: members, a weekly goal, leaderboards, events and a chat. A Clique is one live session that starts, runs and finishes, with everyone's progress visible to each other while it does.
Does it drain my battery?
A GPS session uses the same location hardware as any tracker, and only while a session is running. Outside a workout the app does not follow you, and a Clique streams positions only for the length of that session.
Can I keep my workouts private but still join a club?
Yes. Hide your workouts and you still belong to your Trybes, post, chat and RSVP to events. Your distance is simply not counted on the leaderboard.
What happens to my data if I leave?
Deleting your account removes it along with your posts, workouts, comments, messages and memberships. Accounts created with Apple are revoked with Apple at the same time.
